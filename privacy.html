Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Shorts Maker is a personal tool that uploads videos created by its developer to the developer’s own YouTube channel. This policy explains what it accesses and why.

Information accessed

When you sign in with Google, Shorts Maker requests permission to upload videos to your YouTube channel (the youtube.upload scope). It does not read your email, contacts, files, viewing history, or any other account data.

How it is used

The permission is used only to upload videos that Shorts Maker has created, along with their title, description and tags. Nothing else is done with your account.

Storage and sharing

The sign-in token is stored only on the computer running Shorts Maker, so it can upload without asking you to sign in each time. Shorts Maker has no server, collects no personal data, does not use cookies or analytics, and does not sell or share any information with anyone.

YouTube and Google

Shorts Maker uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can revoke Shorts Maker’s access at any time from your Google account permissions page. Deleting the stored token file on the computer also removes access.
