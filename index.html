Shorts Maker

A personal tool that turns short science-fact scripts into vertical videos and uploads them to its owner’s own YouTube channel.

What it does

Shorts Maker writes a short script about a science fact, adds an AI voiceover, stock footage and captions, and produces a vertical video. It then uploads that video to the YouTube channel of the person running it, using the YouTube Data API.

Who uses it

It is a private tool used only by its developer to publish videos to their own channel. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.
